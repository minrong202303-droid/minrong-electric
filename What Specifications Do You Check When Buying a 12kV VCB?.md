
When comparing 12kV vacuum circuit breakers, I think the biggest mistake is looking only at the price.

For a medium-voltage application, I'd start by checking the basic electrical ratings:

Rated voltage
Rated current
Short-circuit breaking current
Making current
Insulation level
Operating mechanism
Control voltage

The short-circuit breaking capacity is particularly important because it needs to be suitable for the fault level of the electrical system.

Installation conditions also matter. An indoor VCB integrated into switchgear has different requirements from equipment used in an outdoor distribution or substation application.

Another thing I'd check is whether the breaker is compatible with the existing switchgear. Mechanical dimensions, mounting arrangements, control circuits, and accessories can all affect installation.

For reference, this [12kV vacuum circuit breaker](https://minrongdianqi.com/products/vs1-12-kv-indoor-vacuum-circuit-breaker/) provides an example of the specifications buyers can compare when evaluating different VCB models.

I'd also ask the supplier about routine testing, quality control, delivery documentation, and technical support. For industrial electrical equipment, these details can matter just as much as the initial quotation.

If you're comparing suppliers, I'd definitely recommend comparing the complete technical specification rather than choosing based on unit price alone.
